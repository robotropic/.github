### Change robots without stopping the line.

Robotropic makes **Harness**, the workspace where your team and AI agents run the robots, models and tools you already have. Every change is checked against your safety limits, rolled out cell by cell, and recorded.

- **Every change passes the gate.** People and AI agents propose changes; a person approves them.
- **Know a new version is better before it goes live.**
- **Cell by cell, with a way back.**
- **A record you can hand to an auditor.**

**Open source is coming.** Guardcell, our gateway between AI agents and robots, and ShopFloorBench, a benchmark of how AI agents behave when they can change factory robots, will be released here under Apache-2.0.
